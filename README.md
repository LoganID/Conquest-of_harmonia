Conquest of Harmonia Introduction:

It is done—after several years, the moment has finally arrived. 
Although I had to sell my house and a kidney, and lost both my wife and child in the process, I have finally managed to complete this project and produce a version that is, at the very least, playable.
We now have something that attempts to emulate a great idol, and I hope it’s close enough for you to enjoy. 
Please keep in mind that the initial levels are rather "boring," as we didn't really need to include tutorial levels. 
A huge thank you to everyone who helped me with this project.
